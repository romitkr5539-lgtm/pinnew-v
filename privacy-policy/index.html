Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Pinviddl is designed around strict data minimization. This Privacy Policy explains what limited technical data is processed when you use our website and API, and how we protect your privacy across the United States, United Kingdom, Canada, Australia, and the European Economic Area.

1. Information we do not collect

  • We do not require account registration, names, email addresses, or Pinterest credentials to use Pinviddl.
  • We do not maintain a database of user-submitted Pinterest URLs or download histories.
  • We do not store copies of downloaded videos, images, or GIFs on permanent storage.
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information to third parties.

2. Ephemeral request processing & security rate limiting

When you paste a Pinterest Pin URL and submit a request, our edge API processes that URL in memory to retrieve publicly accessible media metadata and stream requested media files to your browser.

To protect the service against automated abuse and denial-of-service traffic, your IP address may be processed temporarily in memory for short-lived rate limiting (typically 60 seconds) and standard Cloudflare edge security telemetry. Public Pin metadata responses may be briefly cached at the edge (for example, up to 5 minutes) without associating them with any user identity.

3. Cookies and local storage

Pinviddl does not set advertising cookies, cross-site tracking cookies, or fingerprinting scripts. Essential network security headers or temporary edge protection cookies may be set by Cloudflare strictly to safeguard network availability.

4. Third-party services

Pinviddl retrieves publicly available media metadata from Pinterest domains and streams files from Pinterest's media CDN (pinimg.com) on your request. Typography assets may be loaded from Google Fonts. Your use of Pinterest itself is governed by Pinterest's own privacy policy.

5. International privacy rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (including GDPR/UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, PIPEDA, and the Australian Privacy Act), you may have rights to inquire about or request deletion of any personal information you voluntarily email to us. Because Pinviddl does not store user accounts or download logs, we hold no personal profile data for standard visitors.

6. Contact

If you have privacy questions, please reach us via our Contact page.
