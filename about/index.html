About Pinviddl

A focused, privacy-first Pinterest media utility built for international web users.

Pinviddl is an independent browser-based tool created to make saving publicly accessible Pinterest videos, images, and GIFs fast, transparent, and distraction-free. Many online downloader websites rely on intrusive pop-ups, misleading download buttons, or heavy scripts. Pinviddl takes the opposite approach: clean HTML, fast CSS, minimal JavaScript, and direct streaming downloads.

How Pinviddl works

When you paste a public Pinterest Pin link (such as pinterest.com/pin/... or a pin.it short URL), our edge API validates that the address belongs strictly to Pinterest, safely resolves official short-link redirects, and inspects only publicly exposed Pin metadata.

  • No account logins: Pinviddl never asks for your Pinterest credentials or accesses private or secret boards.
  • No stored history: We do not maintain a database of user-submitted Pin URLs or downloaded files.
  • Honest format reporting: If a Pin does not expose a direct downloadable video or image, Pinviddl reports the limitation clearly instead of failing silently.
