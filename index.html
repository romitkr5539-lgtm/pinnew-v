How to download from Pinterest

Four straightforward steps to save publicly available Pin media on desktop, iPhone, or Android.

  1. 01

    Open Pinterest

    Find the public video, image, carousel, or GIF Pin you want to save in the Pinterest website or mobile app.

  2. 02

    Copy the Pin URL

    Select Share and tap Copy Link in the app, or copy the full pinterest.com/pin/ address from your browser bar.

  3. 03

    Paste the URL

    Paste the link into Pinviddl above using the Paste button or keyboard shortcut, then click Download to inspect the Pin.

  4. 04

    Download available media

    Review the preview, format, and resolution, then click the Download button to save the file directly to your device.

Frequently asked questions

Practical answers about supported formats, mobile usage, and why certain links cannot be downloaded.

How to download Pinterest videos?

Open the public video Pin on Pinterest and copy its link (either a full pinterest.com/pin/ URL or a pin.it share link). Paste it into Pinviddl, click Download, wait for the preview to load, and then select Download MP4.

How to download Pinterest images?

Copy the Pin link for any public image on Pinterest and paste it into the input box above. Pinviddl looks up the original image source whenever Pinterest exposes it, shows the image dimensions and format, and lets you save the file directly.

Can I download Pinterest GIFs?

Yes. When a Pin serves an original animated .gif file, Pinviddl detects it as a GIF and lets you download it in GIF format. Some animated Pins on Pinterest are re-encoded by Pinterest as looping MP4 videos; in those cases, Pinviddl provides the MP4 download.

Is Pinviddl free?

Yes, Pinviddl is completely free to use with no sign-up, subscription, or watermark added to your downloads.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. Pinviddl runs in mobile web browsers on iPhone (Safari), Android (Chrome, Firefox, Edge), and tablets. In the Pinterest mobile app, tap Share, choose Copy Link, and paste that pin.it URL into Pinviddl.

Why is my Pinterest link unsupported?

Pinviddl only accesses publicly exposed Pin metadata and does not bypass login screens or privacy settings. A link may be unsupported if the Pin belongs to a private or secret board, has been deleted, points to a user profile or board rather than an individual Pin, or is only delivered as a segmented HLS stream without a direct MP4 file.
