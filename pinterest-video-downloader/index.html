How Pinterest video extraction works

When you submit a public Pinterest video URL, Pinviddl resolves any official pin.it short-link redirects and checks the Pin's publicly exposed metadata for direct MP4 video renditions (such as 720p HD or original aspect-ratio clips).

Once verified, the video is displayed in a standard HTML5 video player so you can confirm it is the exact clip you need before saving it. Clicking Download MP4 streams the file directly from Pinterest's official media CDN with a clean filename based on the Pin title.

When a video Pin cannot be downloaded

Pinviddl respects platform access boundaries and does not circumvent private boards, login walls, or DRM controls. In addition, some video Pins are served exclusively as adaptive HLS (.m3u8) playlists without a standalone MP4 file; when that occurs, Pinviddl clearly informs you rather than offering a broken download.

Video downloader questions

What video format does Pinviddl download?

Pinviddl downloads videos in standard MP4 format whenever Pinterest exposes a direct MP4 stream, which plays natively on Windows, macOS, iOS, and Android.

Are audio tracks preserved in Pinterest video downloads?

Yes. Because Pinviddl downloads the direct MP4 file served by Pinterest's CDN without re-encoding, any audio track included in the original Pin video remains intact.

Can I download the video thumbnail as well?

When a video Pin also exposes a high-resolution cover image, Pinviddl lists it alongside the MP4 download options so you can save the cover image separately if needed.
