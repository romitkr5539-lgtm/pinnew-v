Native GIFs vs. looping MP4 Pins on Pinterest

Right-clicking an animated Pin on Pinterest often saves only a static JPEG cover frame rather than the moving animation. Pinviddl reads the Pin's public metadata to locate the actual animated file.

If the original uploader's .gif asset is exposed on Pinterest's CDN, Pinviddl identifies it as a GIF and downloads the animated .gif file directly. If Pinterest transcoded the animation into a looping MP4 video instead, Pinviddl accurately labels and delivers the MP4 file.

GIF downloader questions

Why does saving from Pinterest directly give me a still JPG?

Pinterest often renders a static preview thumbnail on top of animated Pins until hovered or clicked. Pinviddl extracts the underlying original animated asset instead of the static preview frame.

Will downloaded GIFs animate on iPhone and Android?

Yes. Native .gif files saved through Pinviddl keep their original frame data and play in modern photo libraries, messaging apps, and browsers.
